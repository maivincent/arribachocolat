This chocolate is a mystery: on Paccari’s website, the Dark Shark Galapagos bar is described as flavored with coffee and salt. Yet the packaging makes no mention of coffee or salt, and neither flavor is detectable on the palate either.

So what exactly is this bar? A pure chocolate that clearly bears the Paccari signature in its texture and flavors. We still find it rather mild, perhaps a bit more herbal. I’m probably influenced by the name, but when I close my eyes, I feel I can sense a subtle coastal, maritime breeze that brings back certain memories of my trip to that extraordinary place.

### Evaluation

_Uniqueness_: 4  
_Finesse_: 4.5  
_Comfort_: 4.5  
_Intensity_: 4.5  
_General impression_: 4.5  

**Final evaluation**: 22/25
