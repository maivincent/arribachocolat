"Hoy es un gran día," it says on the packaging when you pull back the cardboard flaps. Today is a great day. 
That’s exactly the feeling this magnificent chocolate evokes, with its rich notes of red wine and cranberries. It’s a chocolate for celebrations.

Note: For the past week, I’ve been wearing braces, which prevent me from snacking between meals. So it’s a little harder for me to enjoy my chocolate bars without planning ahead. This bar reminded me why it’s all worth it!
