A fairly thin bar with a smooth surface: this design complements the refined flavor profile of this chocolate. 
What stands out first is a woody flavor—the scent of freshly cut wood, whose fibers form a soft fuzz that makes it smooth to the touch.
Deeper notes reveal hints of tobacco and mushrooms, and if you let it linger in your mouth long enough, you can detect a subtle hint of black cherry acidity.

### Evaluation

_Uniqueness_: 4.5  
_Finesse_: 4.5  
_Comfort_: 4.5  
_Intensity_: 4.5  
_General impression_: 4.5  

**Final evaluation**: 22.5/25
